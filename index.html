      Guide

      Everything you need, in a few minutes. Your entries stay on this phone – nobody else can see them.

      1Put it on your home screen

      iPhone: open the link in Safari, tap the Share button (the square with an arrow), then Add to Home Screen.

      Android: open the link in Chrome, tap ⋮ (top right), then Add to Home screen or Install app.

      Always open it from the icon. On iPhone the icon keeps its own entries – the same link opened in Safari starts empty.

      2Start

      Tap Start, then fill in your name, currency, daily food budget, how you want to split your money (Spend / Save / Invest – 60 / 20 / 20 is a good start), and the money you have right now. That's it.

      3Today – how am I doing?

      • The big number – what's left of today's food budget. The ring fills as you eat.
      • Logging streak – days in a row you logged at least one meal. The dots are this week: green = within budget, amber = over, empty = not logged.
      • This week's wins, money at a glance, and an amber note when something needs doing.

      4Add a meal or anything else

      Tap the green +. Choose what it is at the top: Meal, Spending, Money in, Savings or Investment. For a meal you can type the price right after the food, e.g. chicken rice 8.5 – the amount fills in by itself.

      Made a mistake? Every list has a pencil (edit) and a bin (delete), and you can Undo a delete.

      5The other pages

      • Food – a calendar of the month (green within budget, amber over). Tap a day to see what you ate.
      • Spending – your categories from biggest to smallest, and every payment.
      • Money – your Spend, Save and Invest pots, your total balance, money in, savings goals and investments.
      • Growth – your balance day by day.
      • More – backups, your settings and this guide.

      6Backups and a new phone

      Your entries live only on this phone. Once a week, tap Back up now (on Today when it reminds you, or in More). Save the file to Google Drive, iCloud Drive / Files, or send it to yourself on WhatsApp.

      New phone? Add the app to the home screen, tap Restore from a backup and pick your latest backup file.

      Questions

      Does it need internet?

      Only the very first time. After that it opens without internet.

      Can anyone else see my numbers?

      No. Nothing is uploaded – your entries are stored on your phone only. That's also why backups matter.

      I deleted the app / cleared Safari or Chrome data.

      Add it to the home screen again and use Restore from a backup. Without a backup the entries can't be brought back.

      I want a spreadsheet of everything.

      More → Export to Excel.

      Can I change my budget or split later?

      Yes – More → You / Money split. Changes apply from today; past days keep the old numbers.
